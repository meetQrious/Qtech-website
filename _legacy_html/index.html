About Qrious Tech Team LLP

Qrious Tech is an enterprise software engineering and AI modernization partner. We specialize in custom enterprise artificial intelligence (AI), machine learning (ML), scalable cloud web architectures, cross-platform mobile app development, and data visualization. Our engineers build high-performance, secure digital products tailored to business growth and digital transformation.

Core Engineering Services

  • Enterprise AI & Machine Learning: Custom LLM integrations, predictive models, generative AI pipelines, and enterprise automation.
  • Full-Stack Web Development: High-performance web applications built using React, Next.js, Node.js, NestJS, and cloud-native backends on AWS and Google Cloud.
  • Mobile App Development: Native iOS and Android development alongside cross-platform solutions in React Native and Flutter.
  • Data Visualization & BI: Business intelligence dashboards, Power BI development, and real-time analytical reporting.
  • UI/UX Design Systems: Product architecture, user journey research, wireframing, and interactive design prototypes.

Industry Expertise

  • Healthcare: Telemedicine platforms, EHR integrations, and HIPAA-compliant patient management systems.
  • FinTech: Secure digital banking, payment processing pipelines, and compliant financial reporting software.
  • Education: Modern learning management systems (LMS) and interactive educational software.
  • Retail & E-Commerce: Omnichannel retail platforms, inventory automation, and high-concurrency online stores.
