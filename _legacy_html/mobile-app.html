5 Most Popular Trends for Mobile App Development

  • By Admin
  • 08 May 2023
Mobile app development is essential for all businesses to sustain themselves in this fast-paced digital age. With the latest upgrades in IoT, VR-AR, AI, and Chatbot technology, developers include out-of-the-box features to improve user experiences. Let us explore and discover the most prevalent trends for mobile app development.

From the report by Google lately, it has been revealed that the list of most downloaded apps from the Play Store includes apps built with IoT, AI, AR-VR, Chatbot technologies, of which most Android users would be unaware. This encourages Android developers to develop a mobile app with an astonishing user experience by including out-of-box features. This results in developers embracing revolutionary mobile app development trends to increase engagement and downloads as a result in order to boost revenue generation.

5 Mobile App Development Trends need to be adopted

  1. Chatbot Integration in Existing Mobile App

    “Chatbots market is growing at a CAGR of 37.11% and it is expected to reach USD 6 Billion by the end of 2023.” The above statistic proves that the Chatbots market is growing with immense speed. To give a stimulating user experience, business apps should be combined with Chatbots, hence providing better customer interaction. Some of the most popular and trending Chatbots in the last 12 months are:

    • Amazon Lex
    • Microsoft Bot Framework (Azure Bot)
    • Acquire
    • ChatterOn.io
    • MobileMonkey
    • Flow XO
    • Aivo
    • Botsify
    • Chatfuel
    • Manychat
  2. Artificial Intelligence in Mobile App Development Market

    Voice-enabled technology has brought a revolution in the artificial intelligence industry. After releasing Google Assistant and Siri, it has become mandatory for iOS/Android developers to embrace AI in their mobile app development process to give a better experience and enhance mobile app capabilities. Businesses can engage efficiently with their customers by using mobile tools which can access real-time insights through machine learning technologies, advanced analytics, and cognitive interfaces.

  3. Machine Learning and Recommendation Engines

    Your business app should stand out in the crowd by recommending users services or products which best suit them. Latest Android and iOS version beautifully leverages machine learning by implementing recommendation engines into mobile apps. Machine learning algorithms utilize big data collected from different resources, recognize complex patterns and extract useful insights

  4. Enhancing Security with Blockchain Technology

    Within a short span of time, blockchain technology has gained huge popularity as of being an infrastructure behind Bitcoins. Not just cryptocurrency will get benefited through blockchain technology but financial ledger apps will be developed for the finance industry. The main goal of using blockchain is to enhance security and incorruptible digital ledgers. These ledgers are shared and highly secured that list out a variety of financial transactions and record transaction history.

  5. Accelerated Mobile Pages (AMP)

    Your audience doesn’t like to wait for more to get the webpage loaded. After Google launched the AMP Project, the online businesses and brand websites have got huge benefits in terms of providing fast webpage loading facilities to users. Accelerated Mobile Pages(AMP) Development regardlessly increases visitors, accelerates content replacement, facilitates mobile search, and enhances ad visibility. AMP adds to a better user experience of web-based cross-platform mobile apps.

Conclusion

In short, popular trends in mobile app development include using tools that work on different platforms, making apps faster with 5G, using AI for better user experiences, prioritizing app security, and embracing progressive web apps. Keeping up with these trends helps developers and businesses stay current and create modern, effective mobile applications.
