How do you address and resolve any bugs or issues identified during testing?

    At Qrious Tech Team LLP, we have a structured process in place to address and resolve any bugs or issues identified during testing, ensuring that our products meet the highest standards of quality and reliability. Our approach includes the following steps:

  • Bug Tracking: We use industry-standard bug tracking tools to systematically record and prioritize identified issues. Each bug is assigned a unique identifier and categorized based on severity, impact, and urgency.
  • Analysis and Reproduction: Our development team analyzes each reported bug to understand its root cause and reproduction steps. By reproducing the issue in a controlled environment, we gain insights into the underlying problem and devise effective solutions.
  • Prioritization: Bugs are prioritized based on their severity and impact on the project timeline and functionality. Critical issues that affect core functionality or user experience are addressed with the highest priority, while minor issues may be scheduled for resolution in subsequent releases.
  • Resolution: Once prioritized, our development team works diligently to resolve identified bugs in a timely manner. We follow best practices in software engineering to implement effective fixes while minimizing the risk of introducing new issues or regressions.
  • Testing and Validation: After implementing bug fixes, we conduct thorough testing and validation to ensure that the issues have been successfully resolved and that no new issues have been introduced. Our quality assurance team performs regression testing to verify that the product functions as expected across various scenarios.
  • Documentation: We maintain detailed documentation of all reported bugs, their resolutions, and any associated changes to the product. This documentation serves as a reference for future development efforts and ensures transparency and accountability in our bug resolution process.
